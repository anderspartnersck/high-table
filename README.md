## ▶ [PLAY IT IN YOUR BROWSER][play]

<sub>That link above is the game. Nothing to install.</sub>

Side-view air hockey on a blacklight table. A Castle Killscreen game by Anders &
Partners LLC.

You are the near knocker. Put the puck in the far goal. The table is fast and the
CPU doesn't miss much.

## Controls

Move the mouse and your knocker follows it. Keyboard is WASD or the arrow keys. A
gamepad works if one is plugged in.

- **TRAP** catches the puck and holds it so you can aim
- **BLAST** is a forward lunge on a cooldown. A head-on clank flips the other
  knocker
- **GET BIG** grows your knocker for a few seconds once it's charged
- **P** pauses

On a pad that's A, B and right trigger, with START to pause.

## The modes

Each one is a link. They match the cabinet launchers exactly.

**[1UP ARCADE][play]** is the quarter-eater and the default. Just play.

**[CAMPAIGN][camp]** saves your progress through the Breakaway Cup: beat each
CPU, win the final, take the trophy.

**[CASTLE RULES][castle]** is the timed variant.

**[JAILBREAK][jail]** unlocks everything, all five tables and every knocker and
puck, without touching your save.

**[2UP SPLIT][twoup]** is two players on one machine.

**[TOP-DOWN][todown]** is the to-go version, built for a phone or a quick game
with a mouse.

**[ATTRACT][attract]** is the cabinet's own arcade loop, if you just want to
watch it run.

**[THE GARAGE][garage]** is the cab collection.

## Using a pad

Put it in X-input mode. On an 8BitDo also set the lever to D-pad. If left and
right work but up and down don't, that's the mode rather than the game.
